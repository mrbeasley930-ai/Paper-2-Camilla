Setting the scene
Aeneas' Trojans and their Etruscan allies are fighting the Latins outside the city of Laurentum. Camilla, a princess of the Volsci, fights for Turnus. As a baby she was dedicated by her father to Diana, the virgin huntress goddess, and was brought up in the wilds as a huntress and warrior. In this passage she is at the height of her success in battle. The lines missing from the text (659–663) compare her to the Amazons of Thrace; 666–675 list her victims.
